# Icon attribution

Facility icons are Lucide icons served online through the public Iconify SVG API.

- Lucide source: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide
- Iconify API: https://iconify.design/docs/api/svg.html
- Lucide license and upstream Feather notices are reproduced in `LUCIDE_LICENSE`.

No personal filesystem paths or local PNGs are used by the dashboard. Facility text remains visible when online icons cannot load. Internet is required for the icon images.

Legacy PNG images are retained as historical repository assets and are not used or relicensed by this change. Their original provenance is undocumented.
